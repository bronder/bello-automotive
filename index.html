The guy you're trusting your ride with

Bello Automotive is Kris — a family shop off Worcester Road in Webster. He's been painting for 18 years: self-taught, started in Philadelphia in 2008, Webster since 2019, PPG-certified.

Certified in paint & body, welding, automotive repair, wiring diagnostics, and diesel engines. The short version: welding, disassembly, paint, clear, and polish all happen under this roof, so quality control never leaves the building.

Every job gets real prep, because the finish is only as good as what's under it. If a job isn't right for the shop, he'll say so.
