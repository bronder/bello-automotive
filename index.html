The Shop

Everything in-house. Nothing outsourced.

Bello Automotive is a locally operated, family-run shop — we treat customers like family, and every job gets real prep work, because the finish is only as good as what's under it.

Kris has been painting for 18 years — self-taught, plus certifications in paint & body, welding, automotive repair, wiring diagnostics, and diesel engines. From welding and disassembly to paint, clear, and polish, the whole job happens under our own roof, so quality control never leaves the building.
