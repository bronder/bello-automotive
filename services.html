Good To Know

The details, up front

No surprises — here's how payment, pickup, and our warranty work.

Payment

50% deposit when you drop off, remaining 50% at completion. We accept cash, card, Venmo, and Zelle.

Jump the Queue

Willing to pre-pay? Pre-paid jobs move up in line for a faster turnaround.

Parts or Whole Vehicles

Bring just the parts or the whole vehicle — dropped off or shipped to the shop. Removal & reinstallation is available for an added fee, quoted per vehicle.

Lifetime Paint Warranty

Lifetime warranty on paint work, done to OEM repair procedures with approved, top-quality materials — never cheap eBay products.
